Our Story

Founded in 2026, TORFIN Solutions began with a simple yet powerful mission: to make quality financial services accessible to everyone. What started as a small lending office has grown into a full-service financial institution serving over 50,000 clients nationwide.

Our founder, Jhon Robert Torres, recognized a gap in the market for personalized, technology-enabled financial services that didn't compromise on human touch. Today, we combine cutting-edge financial technology with experienced advisors to deliver solutions that truly meet our clients' needs.

We're proud to be a locally-rooted company with global capabilities, maintaining strong community ties while offering sophisticated financial products typically found only at larger institutions.
